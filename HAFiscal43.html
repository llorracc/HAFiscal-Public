With the revised household block, the un-smoothed rule places the economy at the boundary of the determinacy region — the delayed bite of the policy rate turns the rule into positive feedback for deviations that alternate at the quarterly frequency — so impulse responses under ρr = 0 exhibit oscillations whose amplitude depends on the numerical discretization rather than on the model.
